## The main idea

- The Constitution sets rules for the government: federal, state, and local.
- Courts call government action "state action." Constitutional rights apply to state action.
- A private company, a private school, or a person is usually not the government. The Constitution does not limit what they do.
- Other laws, passed by Congress or your state, often add protections in those places.

## Where your constitutional rights apply

- Police and sheriffs
- Public schools and public colleges
- Government offices and courts
- The government as your employer, like a city, a state, or the post office
- Immigration agents and other federal agents

## Where they usually don't

| Who | Does the Constitution apply? | Laws that may still protect you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A private employer | No | Federal law bans firing for race, color, religion, sex, national origin, or disability, and for age if you are 40 or older. It covers most employers with 15 or more workers. Labor law protects talking with coworkers about pay. |
| A social media app | No | The app's own rules. A few state laws. |
| A store or restaurant | No | Federal and state laws ban turning you away for race, color, religion, or national origin. The ADA bans turning you away for a disability. |
| A landlord | No | Fair housing laws ban discrimination in renting. |
| A private school or college | No | Civil rights laws, and the school's own rules. |

## One exception

- The 13th Amendment bans slavery and forced labor by anyone, not only the government.
- It has one exception: work required as punishment for a crime.
- Federal law makes forced labor, including forcing someone to work off a debt, a crime.

## Why this matters

- Knowing who you are dealing with tells you which rules apply.
- If a police officer stops you, your constitutional rights apply. See the [situation cards](/situations/).
- If your boss or an app treats you unfairly, the answer is usually in a law, not the Constitution. See [At work](/situations/at-work/) and [Posting online](/situations/posting-online/).
