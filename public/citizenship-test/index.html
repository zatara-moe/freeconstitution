Citizenship test practice

Practice the citizenship civics test

All 128 questions from the 2025 test, with the official answers. Practice out loud, one question at a time.

About 10 min Read

The one-line version

The officer asks up to 20 of these 128 questions out loud. Answer 12 correctly to pass.

Which test do I take?

  • Filed Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025: you take the 2025 test on this page.
  • Filed before October 20, 2025: you take the 2008 test of 100 questions. Find it at uscis.gov/citizenship.
  • 65 or older with a green card for 20 years or more: study only the 20 questions marked 65/20. The officer asks 10, and you need 6 right. You may take the test in the language of your choice.
  • For questions about who holds an office now, answer with the person serving on the day of your interview.
  • Words in parentheses are optional. You can say the answer with or without them.

Practice a test

Say each answer out loud before you check it. The real test is spoken. Nothing is saved.

Common questions

How many questions are on the citizenship civics test?

For applications filed on or after October 20, 2025, there are 128 possible questions. The officer asks up to 20 of them out loud. You need 12 correct answers to pass.

What is the 65/20 rule?

If you are 65 or older and have had a green card for at least 20 years, you study only 20 marked questions. The officer asks 10 of them, and you need 6 correct answers.

Which civics test will I take?

It depends on when you filed Form N-400. If you filed on or after October 20, 2025, you take the 2025 test on this page. If you filed before that date, you take the 2008 test of 100 questions.
